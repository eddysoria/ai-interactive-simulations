Selection logic and commands follow the UCLA OARC guide “What statistical analysis should I use?” (SPSS · R · Stata · SAS). Python uses pandas (pd), scipy.stats (st), statsmodels (sm, smf) and, where noted, scikit-learn. Variable names in the commands are generic (y = outcome, x = numerical predictor, g = group). Explanations and work-climate examples are illustrative; no data are shown. The guide is a starting point: the final choice also depends on the design, the sample and the assumptions you check.
