Panels 1–4 show forward propagation; panel 5 trains the neuron. “Aurora-fitted weights” (0.72, −0.17, −2.12) come from a logistic regression on the 5,000 guests of the synthetic Aurora v3 dataset (outcome: returned_12m; average return rate 23%). A single neuron with a sigmoid activation is a logistic regression. Training uses plain (full-batch) gradient descent on the log-loss and converges to these same weights. Inputs are rescaled before entering the neuron: x₁ = satisfaction − 4, x₂ = distance in thousands of km. GELU uses the standard tanh approximation.
