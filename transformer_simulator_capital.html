Toy decoder-only Transformer trained with PyTorch (Adam, cross-entropy) on the 20 sentences listed in step 0. Vocabulary of 20 words, d = 4, one attention head, feed-forward with 8 hidden ReLU units, sinusoidal positions, output layer tied to the embedding matrix (as in GPT-2). Layer normalisation and the attention output projection are left out to keep the arithmetic readable. A real GPT does exactly these operations, only with thousands of dimensions, dozens of heads and dozens of layers.
